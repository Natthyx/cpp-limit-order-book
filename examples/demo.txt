# Build a small book for AAPL (prices are ticks: 10050 = 100.50)
LIMIT SELL AAPL 10100 50
LIMIT SELL AAPL 10100 30
LIMIT SELL AAPL 10200 100
LIMIT BUY  AAPL 9900 40
LIMIT BUY  AAPL 9800 60
BOOK AAPL

# A buy that crosses: trades at the resting price, FIFO within the level
LIMIT BUY AAPL 10100 70
BOOK AAPL

# Price improvement: the buyer was willing to pay 10500 but trades at 10200
LIMIT BUY AAPL 10500 20
BOOK AAPL

# Time-in-force
LIMIT BUY AAPL 10200 500 IOC
LIMIT BUY AAPL 10200 10  FOK
MARKET SELL AAPL 25
BOOK AAPL

# Cancel and modify
LIMIT BUY AAPL 9700 10
CANCEL AAPL 11
CANCEL AAPL 11
MODIFY AAPL 4 9950 40
BOOK AAPL

# Independent books per symbol
LIMIT SELL MSFT 30000 5
SYMBOLS

# Errors are reported, never crash
LIMIT BUY AAPL 100 0
LIMIT BUY AAPL abc 5
